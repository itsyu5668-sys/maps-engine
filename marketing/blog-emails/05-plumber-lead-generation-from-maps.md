# Plumber Lead Generation: Finding Emails from Maps Listings

Plumbers are a great B2B target. They have high ticket jobs, they run real businesses with real websites, and they are easy to find on Google Maps in any city. The challenge is getting a contact email that actually reaches someone.

Here is how I build plumber email lists from Maps data.

## Why plumbers are a good vertical

The average plumbing service call is $300 to $800, and a repipe or water heater job is $2,000 to $8,000. Plumbers have cash flow, which means they have budget for software, marketing, and tools. They are also independent, which means the person reading info@ is usually the owner or the office manager. No gatekeeper.

## The query

Run "plumbers in Phoenix with emails". You get back every plumber Maps knows about in Phoenix, each with a public email scraped from their own website.

## Real numbers from a Phoenix run

- Plumbers found on Maps: 20
- With a website: 20
- With a findable public email: 11
- Skipped, no public email: 9
- Hit rate: 55 percent

Plumbers actually have a higher email hit rate than roofers in my testing, around 50 to 60 percent. They tend to have cleaner websites with dedicated contact pages.

## What you get

Each lead:

- name
- address
- rating
- phone
- website
- email

The email is scraped from the plumber's own site, usually an info@ or office@ address on their contact page. No guessing, no pattern matching.

## The qualify step

Plumbers have a wider quality spread than roofers. Some are 5.0 with 500 reviews. Some are 3.2 with 12 reviews. Filter hard.

**Drop anything under 4.0.** A plumber with bad reviews has bigger problems than your offer.

**Drop one person shops with no website.** They are too small for most B2B products.

**Keep the mid market.** 4.2 to 4.8 rating, 50 to 300 reviews, real website, public email. That is your buyer.

## The email

Plumbers respond to operational pain. Their biggest problems are after hours calls, dispatch, and quoting. Reference those.

A real send: "Saw your 4.8 rating on Maps. Most plumbers I talk to in Phoenix lose after hours calls to voicemail because they do not have an answering service. I built one for trades. Want a 5 minute demo Tuesday?"

## Cost

At $2 per 1,000 results and a 55 percent email hit rate, your cost per plumber email lead is about $3.60. For a plumbing software retainer at $500/month, you need one close per 100 sends to be profitable. That is a 1 percent close rate, which is very achievable in this vertical.

## What to skip

Do not bother with national plumbing franchises for email outreach. Their local sites usually route to a corporate contact form, and the email hit rate drops below 20 percent. Stick to independent plumbers with their own websites.
