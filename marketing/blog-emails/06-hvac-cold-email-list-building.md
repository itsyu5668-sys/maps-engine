# Cold Email List Building for HVAC Companies

HVAC is a seasonal vertical, which makes it tricky for cold email. If you pitch HVAC companies in July in Phoenix, they are too busy to read your email. If you pitch them in October, they are planning for winter and have time to evaluate tools. Timing plus good list data is the whole game.

Here is how I build HVAC email lists from Maps data and time the sends.

## The query

Run "HVAC contractors in Atlanta with emails". You get every HVAC company Maps knows about in Atlanta, each with a public email scraped from their own website.

## Atlanta numbers

- HVAC companies found: 20
- With a website: 20
- With a findable public email: 11
- Skipped, no public email: 9
- Hit rate: 55 percent

HVAC companies run clean websites. They know homeowners check reviews before booking, so they invest in their web presence. That means a public contact email is usually there.

The email is scraped from their own contact page. Real and public.

## Timing the send

HVAC has two busy seasons: summer cooling and winter heating. Pitch them in the shoulder seasons.

- For southern markets like Atlanta, Phoenix, Dallas: pitch October to November and February to March.
- For northern markets: pitch April to May and September to October.

If you pitch during peak season, your reply rate drops by half. They are not ignoring you, they are on a roof.

## Qualify the list

HVAC companies range from one truck operations to 50 truck fleets. Filter by signals:

**Rating.** Keep 4.0 and above.

**Reviews.** Under 20 reviews usually means too small. Over 500 usually means a larger fleet with an office manager. Both can buy, but the pitch differs.

**Email type.** info@ and office@ go to an inbox someone reads. service@ sometimes goes to a dispatch system. If you only get a service@ email, follow up with a phone call too.

## The email

HVAC owners care about dispatch efficiency, after hours calls, and recurring maintenance contracts. Reference the season.

A real send in October: "Saw your 4.7 rating on Maps. Most Atlanta HVAC companies I talk to are booking winter tune ups right now but losing the upsell to full system installs. I built a quoting tool that fixes that. Worth a call this week?"

## Cost per lead

At $2 per 1,000 results and 55 percent email hit rate, your cost per HVAC email lead is about $3.60. HVAC retainers run $1,000 to $3,000 per month for software or marketing services, so the unit economics work even at a 1 percent close rate.
