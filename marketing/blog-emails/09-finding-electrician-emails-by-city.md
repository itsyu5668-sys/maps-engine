# Finding Electrician Emails by City for Outreach

Electricians are an underrated B2B vertical. They have steady work, they run real websites, and they are easy to find on Google Maps in every mid size city. If you sell to trades, electricians should be on your list alongside roofers and plumbers.

Here is how I build electrician email lists by city.

## The query

Run "electricians in Denver with emails". You get every electrician Maps knows about in Denver, each with a public email scraped from their own website.

## Denver numbers

- Electricians found on Maps: 20
- With a website: 20
- With a findable public email: 10
- Skipped, no public email: 10
- Hit rate: 50 percent

Electricians sit right in the middle for email hit rate. About half publish a public email. The other half use a contact form or list only a phone number.

## Why city targeting matters

Electricians are licensed at the state or city level, so they are inherently local. You cannot sell to a national list the way you can with SaaS companies. You have to go city by city.

The good news: every mid size US city has 50 to 200 electricians on Maps. That is enough volume to build a real list in any market you target.

Pick cities by your offer:

- If you sell marketing services, target growing cities where electricians are competing for new construction work.
- If you sell software, target cities with a lot of commercial electrical work, where electricians have fleets and dispatch needs.
- If you sell insurance or financing, target older cities with a lot of residential rewiring.

## Qualify before you send

Electricians have a wider range than roofers. Some are one person shops doing small residential jobs. Some are 30 person commercial crews. Filter:

**Rating.** Keep 4.0 and above.

**Reviews.** Under 15 reviews is usually too small. Over 200 is usually a bigger commercial operation.

**Website quality.** A clean, fast site with a real contact page means a business that invests. A slow, broken site means a business that does not.

**Email type.** info@ and contact@ are best. A personal name email (john@) sometimes means the owner reads everything, which is great for outreach.

## The email

Electricians care about scheduling, quoting, and code compliance. Reference those.

A real send: "Saw your 4.8 rating on Maps. Most electricians I talk to in Denver are quoting jobs by hand and losing the ones where the customer gets 3 bids. I built a quoting tool that turns that into a 5 minute job. Worth a look Wednesday?"

## Scaling across cities

Once you have a workflow for one city, it is the same query with a different city name. I run the same scrape across 10 cities in an afternoon and get a 500 electrician list with 250 emails. That is a full quarter of cold email leads for a trades focused B2B offer.
